About MrButler

Competitive Fortnite, creator growth, and a career built across multiple platforms.

His Story

Emery “MrButler” Butler is an American competitive Fortnite player and content creator. Since 2022, he has built his platform through livestreaming, competitive gameplay, short-form content, and a growing community across multiple social platforms.

MrButler competes across Fortnite modes and tournaments while continuing to grow as a creator. He has reached Unreal rank 44 times and is currently a Kick Partner.

After previous chapters with OneUp Esports and 7VEN Club, MrButler signed with RXB Esports as a Fortnite pro and content creator.
